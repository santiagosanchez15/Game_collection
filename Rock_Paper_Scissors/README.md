# Rock Paper Scissors 

## Project Description
basic child game, where you look to beat your opponent. 

**Set of rules**
1. The game is played with two people
2. There are three outcomes: WIN, LOOSE, DRAW
3. ROCK beats SCISSORS
4. SCISSORS beats PAPER 
5. PAPER beats SCISSORS

## New additions
To make the game more interseting I have decided to add money, and bets. 
- If the player wins doubles its bet if it loses, loses the money it bet
- If the player doesn't have enough money loses
- The player can stop playing whenver they want
